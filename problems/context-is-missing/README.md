# Context Is Missing

Use this family when a person or system cannot make, explain, or safely review a decision because important information is absent.

## What engineers notice

- decisions depend on knowledge held by one person or system
- an agent produces plausible but poorly grounded answers
- incident investigation cannot reconstruct the conditions of a decision

## Questions to ask first

1. What decision cannot be made or explained?
2. Which facts, constraints, or history are missing at that point?
3. Where is the missing context created, and how can it be made available safely?
