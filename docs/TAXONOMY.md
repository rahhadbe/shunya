# Problem Taxonomy

SHUNYA is organized by the problem an engineer notices, not the technology they happen to use.

Choose the closest family. A case may be linked from more than one family when that genuinely improves discovery.

| Problem family | Use it when the main question is |
| --- | --- |
| `something-is-slow` | Why does this take too long or process too little? |
| `something-is-inconsistent` | Why do related views, records, or outcomes disagree? |
| `something-is-unreliable` | Why does this fail intermittently or under normal operation? |
| `something-is-expensive` | Why does this consume too much money, compute, storage, or effort? |
| `things-are-competing` | What shared resource is limiting or blocking work? |
| `something-is-nondeterministic` | Why can the same conditions produce different outcomes? |
| `something-is-failing-halfway` | How should a multi-step operation behave after partial completion? |
| `data-is-stale` | Why is the information no longer current enough? |
| `context-is-missing` | What information is needed to make or explain a decision? |
| `ai-is-wrong` | Why is an AI-produced answer or inference incorrect? |
| `ai-is-taking-the-wrong-action` | Why did an AI system choose an unsafe or unsuitable action? |
| `change-is-dangerous` | What makes a change risky to deploy, migrate, or reverse? |
| `failure-cannot-be-reproduced` | What is missing to recreate and understand a failure? |
| `knowledge-is-lost` | What important decision, constraint, or history is disappearing? |

## Rules

- Name a family after a durable problem, not a product, company, or technique.
- Add a family only after a real case does not fit the current set.
- Prefer a broad family with clear links over many narrow categories.
- Use tags and links inside a case for secondary concerns; do not force a new directory for every variation.
